# Quick Baguettes

Four ingredients, no kneading, a long overnight rise, and a hot oven. Mix it before bed, shape and bake in the morning.

## Ingredients

- 950g (8.5 cups) baker's or bread flour — higher protein, ~11.5g per 100g
- 2 heaped tsp salt (~16g)
- 3/4 tsp yeast (~2g)
- 730ml (3.1 cups) filtered water, room temperature

## Instructions

### Part 1

1. Mix all the ingredients together until combined. The dough will come together into a ball — no more than 2 minutes.
2. Cover and let it rise for 8 to 10 hours. The cold oven is a good place for it.

### Part 2

3. Sprinkle flour on the bench or a pizza dish, scrape the dough out, and sprinkle more flour on top to stop sticking. Use a plastic dough scraper and don't press the dough — you want to keep the air in.
4. Cut the dough into 4 (or 2, 3, or 5, depending on how many baguettes you want).
5. Gently stretch each piece into a baguette shape and place on a baking tray lined with baking paper. Shaping should take about 3 minutes, cleaning included.
6. Bake at 250°C (485°F) in a preheated oven for around 25 minutes, depending on how golden you like them.

## Notes

- The long rise does the work that kneading would. Don't shortcut it.
- Handle the shaped dough lightly — pressing it out flattens the open crumb you've been waiting for.
