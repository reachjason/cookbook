# Charred Corn, Black Bean & Avocado Salad

Blistered corn, black beans and big chunks of avocado, dressed with lime and cumin. The base can be made ahead; the avocado goes in at the last minute.

## Ingredients

- 2 large ripe avocados
- 300g corn kernels (fresh or frozen)
- 400g black beans, drained and rinsed
- 200g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 small red onion, finely sliced
- 1 red chilli, deseeded and finely chopped
- 25g fresh coriander, roughly chopped
- 2 limes, juiced
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

## Instructions

1. **Char the corn.** Heat a dry heavy frying pan over high heat until very hot. Add the corn in a single layer and leave it alone for 8 minutes so it blisters, then toss and repeat until you have dark spots across most of the kernels. Tip onto a plate to cool.

2. **Soften the onion.** While the corn cooks, put the sliced red onion in a small bowl with the lime juice and a pinch of the salt. Leave it to sit — the acid takes the raw bite out and turns the slices pink.

3. **Make the dressing.** Whisk the olive oil, cumin, remaining salt and the pepper into the onion and lime juice. Taste and add more lime if it needs sharpening.

4. **Build the base.** In a large bowl, combine the black beans, cherry tomatoes, chilli, most of the coriander and the cooled corn. Pour over the dressing and toss well.

5. **Fold in the avocado.** Halve, stone and dice the avocados into large chunks. Add to the bowl and fold gently — two or three turns only, so the pieces stay intact. Scatter with the reserved coriander and serve.

## Notes

- Make the base up to a day ahead, but fold in the avocado just before serving so it stays green.
- Crumbled feta or cotija is a good addition if you're not keeping it dairy free.
- If you're cooking for someone with coeliac disease, check the canned beans — plain beans in water are fine, but seasoned or chilli-style versions sometimes contain wheat starch.
